# Zelda BOTW

An open-world action game built from scratch in one HTML file, in the shape of
*Breath of the Wild*'s Hyrule. Everything here — terrain, models, sounds and
music — is generated in code. No game assets are used.

Open `index.html` in a browser. It needs an internet connection, because it
pulls three.js from a CDN.

## What's in it

- **260 hand-authored terrain passes** across a 10 km Hyrule: 15 regions, from
  Death Mountain at 751 m to the Hebra peaks at 995 m.
- **120 shrines**, 107 of them using their real trial name and mechanic —
  Halt the Tilt, Myahm Agana Apparatus, Trial of Power, Metal Makes a Path.
- **900 Korok seeds** with four puzzle kinds, and Hestu to trade them to.
- **All four Divine Beasts**; Vah Ruta is boardable with five terminals.
- **Calamity Ganon** in the castle, then Dark Beast Ganon in the field.
- 108 armour pieces including DLC and amiibo, placed where they are really found.
- 97 NPCs, shops with a rupee economy, 30 wild horses, four Great Fairies.
- The real numbers: enemy HP and damage, the 1000-unit stamina wheel,
  `attack + weapon − defence`, a 24-minute day.

## Controls

WASD move · Shift sprint · Space jump/climb/paraglide · Ctrl sneak ·
Left click attack (hold to charge) · Right click aim bow · Z shield ·
Z+A/D sidehop · Z+S backflip · G throw weapon · F rune · R cycle rune ·
E interact/mount · 1-4 swap gear · Tab arrow type · C eat/cook ·
H/J/K Champion abilities · M map · Esc inventory
